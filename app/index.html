01 | EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This project establishes a structured digital inventory of campus facilities using smartphone-based location capture and GIS-compatible data. Each mapped feature can store its geometry, asset identifier, category, condition, descriptive attributes, photographs and GPS observations. The resulting dataset can be exported as GeoJSON and CSV for further processing in QGIS.

02 | PROJECT OBJECTIVES

• Map academic blocks, laboratories, library and support facilities.
• Record campus roads, paths, parking, drainage and utilities.
• Build a georeferenced asset register using WGS 84 coordinates.

• Capture site photographs and field attributes.
• Calculate indicative line lengths and polygon areas.
• Review GPS elevation observations and prepare GIS outputs.

05 | METHODOLOGY

1. Define the feature category and geometry type (point, line or polygon). 2. Capture GPS positions at feature locations or along boundaries. 3. Enter asset attributes and attach site photographs. 4. Calculate indicative lengths and areas from geographic coordinates. 5. Export the spatial dataset and attributes for quality review and cartographic production in QGIS.

06 | DATA QUALITY, LIMITATIONS & RECOMMENDATIONS

Smartphone GNSS accuracy varies with device, satellite geometry, buildings, trees and atmospheric conditions. Smartphone altitude is not survey-grade and may contain significant vertical error. Polygon area and route length are indicative until validated against control points. Satellite imagery is a visual reference and may have positional offsets or acquisition dates that differ from the field survey. For engineering-grade outputs, establish ground control and validate measurements using RTK GNSS, total station or differential levelling. Record date, device, accuracy and field notes for each observation.

07 | SOFTWARE & DATA DELIVERY

Mobile field application: MIT Campus Atlas • Coordinate reference: WGS 84 (EPSG:4326) • GIS processing target: QGIS • Data delivery: GeoJSON, CSV and JSON backup. Basemap imagery is streamed online and is not embedded in the offline database.
