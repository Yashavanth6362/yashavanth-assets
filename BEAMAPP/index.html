About BeamPro
BeamPro FEM

A beam-analysis learning and preliminary engineering tool using the direct stiffness method with Euler-Bernoulli beam elements. It models supports and loads, then presents reactions, shear, moment and deflection, with an optional satellite site pin and public site data.

Created byYASHAVANTH AN
DisciplineCivil Engineering
How to use
  1. Enter beam length, E, I and section depth.
  2. Pick a support preset or add supports.
  3. Optionally pin the site on the map and fetch site data.
  4. Add loads (dead or live) and choose load factors.
  5. Open Results to review diagrams and checks.
  6. Save the project and export the report.
Engineering responsibility and limitations

Linear-elastic, small-deflection, constant EI, shear deformation neglected. Checks cover extreme-fibre bending stress and deflection only; shear, stability, connections, vibration, fire, durability, foundations and code requirements are not checked. Site data is indicative and not a ground investigation. A qualified engineer must verify results before construction or safety-critical use.
